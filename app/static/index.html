03 / METHOD

From natural language to an executable plan

01

Interpret

LLM-assisted interpretation turns operator notes into structured directives such as solar reduction, reserve requirements and no-charge windows.

02

Optimize

A constrained linear program balances demand, solar, grid supply and battery movement while minimizing grid energy cost.

03

Validate

The generated schedule is checked against request constraints before it is returned as the final plan.
